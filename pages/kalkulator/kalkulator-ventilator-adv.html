⚙️ Ventilator Lanjutan — Compliance · Driving P · Mechanical Power
📌 Formula & Target Klinis
  • Compliance statis = VT ÷ (Pplat − PEEP) · Target >50 mL/cmH₂O
  • Driving Pressure = Pplat − PEEP · Target ≤15 cmH₂O
  • Mechanical Power = 0.098 × RR × VT × (Ppeak − ΔP/2) ÷ 1000 · Target <17 J/min
  • Resistensi = (Ppeak − Pplat) × Ti ÷ VT · Ti estimasi = 60 / (RR × 3)
  • VT target: ARDS 4–6, PPOK 7–8, Normal 6–8 mL/kg IBW
  • Pplat target: ≤30 cmH₂O (ARDS), ≤35 (non-ARDS)
  • PEEP: ARDSNet lower/higher PEEP-FiO₂ table
  • Inspiratory hold 0.5 dtk untuk ukur Pplat (di VC-AC mode)
